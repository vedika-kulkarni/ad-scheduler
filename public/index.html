Algorithm Info
Greedy — Activity Selection
Sorts ads by end time, then greedily picks non-overlapping ads.
Time: O(n log n) · Optimal for maximizing count.
Greedy — Bid/Duration Ratio
Picks ads with highest bid ÷ duration ratio first.
Time: O(n²) · Good for revenue density.
DP — Weighted Interval Scheduling
Finds optimal non-overlapping set maximizing total bid.
Time: O(n log n) · Provably optimal for interval scheduling.
DP — 0/1 Knapsack
Treats each ad's duration as weight, bid as value.
Time: O(n × capacity) · Optimal for time-capacity constraint.
